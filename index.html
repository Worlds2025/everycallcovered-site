      Questions people ask first

      The fastest way to judge the voice is the demo call. It rings your phone.

      Does it sound like a robot?

      It talks in short turns at a normal pace, stops when the caller cuts in, and doesn't repeat the same question word for word. It never says "as an AI."

      It also never pretends to be a person. If a caller asks, it says it's your virtual assistant.

      Can I keep my phone number?

      Yes. You get a new local number for the line, and you forward your current number to it. Setup shows the forwarding steps for Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, office and home lines, plus a way that works with any phone company.

      Nothing is ported, and no phone-company PIN is needed.

      What happens in an emergency?

      You choose what counts as an emergency, starting from your trade's list, and who's on call, in order. When a caller describes one, the receptionist gives the safety step first, checks it's happening now, and takes their name, number and address.

      Then it calls your first on-call person, who presses 1 to take it. If they don't answer within about a minute, it tries the next person. It never promises the caller an arrival time.

      By default this applies after hours. You can turn it on for any time of day.

      What if I run out of minutes or credit?

      On Business and Growth, calls past your included minutes use prepaid credit at $0.39 a minute. You get an email when credit runs low, and auto top-up can add more for you. If minutes and credit both run out, callers reach a plain voicemail until you top up.

      On Personal, every call uses credit at $0.45 a minute. When about a minute is left on a call, your assistant says so, and it ends the call politely when credit runs out.

      Can a caller talk to a real person?

      Yes, if you want that. Turn on transfers, and during your hours the receptionist takes the caller's name and number, then rings the phones you chose. If nobody can pick up, it takes a message.

      Your team can also pass any live call to each other, warm or blind.

      Are calls recorded?

      Every answered call starts with a recording notice. If a caller says they don't want to be recorded, the recording is deleted.

      Recordings play from the call's page, next to the transcript, and are kept for a year. You can delete one sooner.

      Will it put jobs on my calendar?

      No. Every request lands in your inbox as "Needs confirmation." You confirm, edit, assign or decline it. Nothing is scheduled on its own, and confirming never texts the customer.

      Is there a free trial?

      No trial, but a free demo. Sign up, tell it how your calls should be answered, and it calls your phone. You play a customer and it plays your receptionist. You get three demo calls of up to three minutes each, and the transcript of each one.
