You can't answer from under a sink.

When you're on a job, driving between jobs, or done for the day, the phone keeps ringing. A missed call is a lost job: the customer calls the next number on the list.

Every Call Covered answers for you, 24/7, and starts with the hours you can't cover: evenings, weekends and holidays. No call goes to a voicemail nobody checks.

How it works

  1. It answers every call

    Your business number rings through to you when you're available. When you're not, the AI receptionist picks up, in your business's name.

  2. It captures the job

    It asks the questions your trade needs, takes a message with a full transcript, and books a job request for you to confirm.

  3. You stay in control

    Confirm, assign or call back from the Android app or the web dashboard. You can take over a live call at any time.

Emergencies break through to a person.

Normal after-hours calls get answered, booked or messaged. When a call is an emergency for your trade, the AI alerts your on-call person right away, by call and text, even in the middle of the night.

You decide what counts as an emergency and who gets the alert.

  • Burst pipe
  • No heat in winter
  • Roof leaking into the house
  • Fridge down and food spoiling
  • Smell of gas: the caller is told to get safe and call for help first

It answers in your caller's language.

The AI recognizes the caller's language in the first seconds and answers in it. It covers the 25 most spoken languages in the US. You see the transcript in the original language and in English.
