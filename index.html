    What it does

    On the call

    • Answers in your nameIt greets callers as your business, knows your hours and services, and plays the recording notice first.
    • Replies in the caller's languageA caller who speaks Spanish hears Spanish back. Your notes stay in English.
    • Asks what your trade needsFor appliance repair: the appliance, the brand, whether it's under warranty, the address and a good time. Each trade asks its own questions.
    • Says what it isIt doesn't pretend to be a person. Asked, it says it's your virtual assistant.
    • Emergencies go to a personYour trade's emergency list, plus your own. It gives the safety step first, checks the problem is happening now, takes the address, then calls your on-call people in order until one presses 1. For a gas smell, it tells the caller to leave and call 911, books nothing, and still alerts your on-call person.

    When you step in

    • Several phones ring at onceChoose who rings: the app open in a browser, a mobile number, or both. The first to answer gets the call.
    • Call back from the appThe owner and office call customers from the browser. Customers see your business number, not a cell.
    • Hold and transferPut a caller on hold with music, or pass the call to a teammate, warm or blind. Try it here.

    Questions

    Does it sound like a robot?

    It talks like a good front desk: short turns at a normal pace, and it stops talking when the caller cuts in.

    The quickest way to judge the voice is the demo call, which rings your own phone.

    Can I keep my phone number?

    Yes. You get a new local number for the line, and you forward your current number to it. Setup shows the forwarding steps for Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, office and home lines, plus general steps for any other phone company.

    Nothing is ported, and no phone-company PIN is needed.

    What happens in an emergency?

    You choose what counts as an emergency, starting from your trade's list, and who's on call, in order. When a caller describes one, the receptionist gives the safety step first, checks it's happening now, and takes their name, number and address.

    Then it calls your first on-call person, who presses 1 to take it. If they don't answer within about a minute, it tries the next person. It never promises the caller an arrival time.

    If your receptionist answers all your calls, emergencies reach your on-call person at any hour. If your team's phones ring first while you're open, the on-call person is called only after hours. You can change this either way.

    A gas smell, smoke or carbon monoxide always alerts your on-call person, day or night.

    What if I run out of minutes or credit?

    On Business and Growth, calls past your included minutes use prepaid credit at $0.39 a minute. You get an email when credit runs low, and auto-refill can add more for you. If minutes and credit both run out, callers reach a plain voicemail until you top up.

    On Personal, every call uses credit at $0.45 a minute. When about a minute is left on a call, your assistant says so, and it ends the call politely when credit runs out.

    Can a caller talk to a real person?

    Yes. Transfers are on unless you turn them off. During your hours the receptionist takes the caller's name and number, then rings the phones you chose. If nobody answers, the caller can leave a voicemail.

    Your team can also pass any live call to each other, warm or blind.

    Are calls recorded?

    Every call your receptionist answers starts with a recording notice, and so does voicemail. Calls your team answers directly aren't recorded.

    If a caller says they don't want to be recorded, nothing is kept. The call ends politely, and you get their number to call back.

    Recordings are kept for a year. The owner plays them from the call's page, next to the transcript, and can delete one sooner.

    Will it put jobs on my calendar?

    No. Every request lands in your inbox as “Needs confirmation.” You confirm, edit, assign or decline it. Nothing is scheduled on its own, and confirming never texts the customer.
